Hi there! I’m José Devienne, a scientist at the Department of Earth Sciences, University of Cambridge. I study magnetic minerals and the records they preserve in terrestrial rocks and meteorites. By combining micromagnetic simulations, statistical modelling, high-performance computing, machine learning, and laboratory experiments, I investigate how these minerals acquire and retain their magnetisation. These magnetic signatures provide valuable insights into the formation and evolution of planetary bodies, the conditions that shape planetary habitability, and the possible role of magnetic minerals and facilitating life's emergence. I am also interested in scaling scientific and machine-learning workflows to HPC and cloud platforms, and in making these tools more accessible through intuitive visual interfaces.
