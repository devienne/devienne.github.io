Hi there! I'm José Devienne, a scientist at the Department of Earth Sciences, University of Cambridge. In my research, I combine numerical modeling, HPC, machine learning, and experimental work to track magnetic records preserved in terrestrial rocks and meteorites. I then use this information to understand how planets formed and evolved, and how Earth's magnetic field may have influenced the origin of life. My recent interests include extending locally developed ML codes to larger-scale facilities (e.g., cloud computing), and exploring how such applications can be delivered efficiently through intuitive visual interfaces.
