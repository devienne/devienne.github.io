ABOUT

I am a scientist with a PhD in Geophysics and 8+ years of experience in computational modeling, data analysis, and scientific programming. My background combines strong quantitative research with hands-on work in Python, machine learning, automation, and scalable data workflows.

Throughout my career, I have developed computational pipelines to process complex datasets, automate simulations, and investigate physical systems using finite-element modeling and data-driven methods. I have also applied machine learning to real-world problems such as landslide classification and earthquake prediction.

I also hold an MSc in Applied Physics and a BSc in Physics. I've recently completed a postgraduate course in ML Engineering, where I've been involved in a number of exciting real-world projects ranging from housing-price analysis, recommendation systems, and forecasting. During this course, a strong emphasis was given on the development of APIs for data extraction and transformation, as well as on deployment of ML models.
